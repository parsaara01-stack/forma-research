# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-17
- Preserved the unmodified v0.1 baseline, then populated all eight pilot companies from live SEC responses.
- Enforced single-filing and annual-duration coherence, CIK identity pins, finite facts and conservative nonoverlapping debt definitions.
- Added respectful retries, bounded caching, hashed raw inputs, immutable snapshots, validation before publication and offline replication.
- Fixed future-fact leakage in stale fallback and prevented unchanged refreshes from rewriting outputs.
- Added source and capital evidence dialogs, partial maturity buckets, dilution inputs, visible stale/missing states and fiscal-end dates.
- Corrected the simulator's misleading stacked cash bridge; preserved its deterministic illustrative assumptions.
- Packaged public research methods and a draft protocol; removed claims of external preregistration.
- Added regression, browser and accessibility checks; gated deployment and twice-weekly data updates on validation.
