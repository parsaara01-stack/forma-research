forma — Small-Cap Research

An ongoing study of small-cap stocks, focused on the Russell 2000 and IWM

When does small-cap growth pay off?

forma asks whether earnings growth and recovery lead to better returns — and how debt, funding costs and new shares affect the outcome

The research focuses on the Russell 2000 and IWM, the ETF that tracks the index

We are starting with 20 companies to check the data process before building a full index study
