# forma. — brand system 0.2

**Brand concept:** research that reveals structure. Inspired by restrained editorial product design, not by copying Apple assets, trade dress or logos.

## Identity
- **Name:** forma. (lowercase, period always). Working brand subject to trademark clearance before commercial use.
- **Research motto:** The economics beneath the ticker.
- **Voice:** measured, economical, exact; never use hype, opaque scoring or unsupported predictive claims.
- **Wordmark:** customized text treatment with a cobalt period and a minimal three-rung mark suggesting capital layers. Original icon designed for this project.
- **Typography:** system font stack (`-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, system sans) to avoid external licensed font redistribution. Tabular figures for numeric outputs.

## Experience principles
1. Typography and space carry hierarchy; no ornamental dashboard clutter.
2. Every chart/unit states what is observed, simulated, estimated, or not available.
3. Research pages are accessible and responsive, keyboard controls work, and reduced-motion preference is honored.
4. Visual trust is earned by provenance, not badges claiming accuracy.
5. No stock price target is displayed without a measured model, assumptions and uncertainty.
6. Static site with minimal dependency footprint loads quickly and can be archived.

## Publication layout
Hero → Perspective → Capital framework → Laboratory simulator → Research questions → Observation panel → Methodology and evidence → Footer.

## Content guidance
Avoid "guaranteed alpha," "predict with certainty," and "AI knows the future." Prefer "tested," "not yet tested," "not available," "simulation," and "as filed on DATE."
