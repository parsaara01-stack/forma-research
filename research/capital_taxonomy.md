# Capital & cost taxonomy (research data map)

| Concept | Operational definition / source | Status | Main caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maintenance capital needs | Disclosed sustaining capex or separately sourced asset replacement cycle | Not collected | Cannot generally derive mechanically from depreciation |
| Growth reinvestment | Acquisitions + productive growth capex + change in noncash working capital + intangible investment | Not collected | Distinguish organically grown assets from purchased assets |
| Capital efficiency | Marginal NOPAT versus incremental invested capital | Planned | Adjust R&D, leases, goodwill, financials |
| Working-capital drag | Δreceivables + Δinventories − Δpayables, scaled by Δsales | Planned | Working-capital cash release may be temporary |
| Operating cash quality | CFO minus capex, accruals, SBC-adjusted dilution, collections | CFO/Capex pilot | Cash taxes, classification and acquisition payments matter |
| Fixed/variable-rate debt | Instrument-level contractual exposure and hedges | Not collected | Effective exposure differs from headline floating amount |
| Debt maturity wall | Principal due 1, 2, 3, 5 years versus liquidity/revolver capacity | Not collected | Must read debt notes; not available as simple standard XBRL number |
| Capital stack & seniority | Secured loans, revolvers, unsecured bonds, leases, convertibles, preferred | Not collected | Seniority changes downside recovery value |
| Marginal cost of financing | New-issue spread/yield, issuance costs, covenants, equity required return | Not collected | Book interest expense / debt is historical, not marginal |
| Cost of equity | Risk-free + empirically estimated beta premia and size/other exposures | Not collected | WACC is model dependent, not filing fact |
| Dilution | Gross issuance, repurchases, SBC and convertibles, relative to shares at t0 | Not collected | Per-share return may differ from enterprise growth |
| Runway | Cash + eligible revolver capacity − restricted cash / modeled burn | Not collected | Cash may be inaccessible; burn nonstationary |
| Nondebt obligations | Operating leases, purchase commitments, pensions, litigation, contingencies | Not collected | Can matter substantially in distress |
| Customer concentration | Disclosed customer, contract/industry exposure | Not collected | Demand concentration magnifies funding shocks |
| Ownership & liquidity | Holder mix, insider trading, equity issuance capacity, bid/ask spread | Not collected | Liquidity shocks may dominate fundamentals |
| Analyst expectations | Timestamped forecasts, revisions, dispersion and consensus methodology | Not collected | Cannot reconstruct historical analyst consensus from today's snapshot |
| Valuation implied expectations | Reverse DCF and price-to-fundamentals relative to capital needs | Not collected | Required discount rates and growth assumptions vary |
| Sector heterogeneity | Financials vs industrials vs energy vs biotech vs REITs | Pilot sector labels | Universal rankings create mechanical misclassification |
| Delisting/survival | Delisting return, bankruptcy, merger consideration | Not collected | Survivorship and selection bias |

Priority order: (i) SEC statement and historical source provenance, (ii) financing disclosures, (iii) point-in-time market/expectation dataset, (iv) pre-registered model comparisons, (v) validated publication. Nulls are required, not defects.
