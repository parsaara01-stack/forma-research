01 / PERSPECTIVE

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

Growth is visible.
Value creation is harder to see.

A 50% earnings forecast says remarkably little about a business without context: what capital does growth require, who finances it, and what remains for existing shareholders?

We investigate the mechanism between capital raised, capital deployed, operating cash generated, and shareholder value realized.

06 / INTEGRITY BY DESIGN

Show the method.
Show the uncertainty.

Every number is only as useful as its source, its information date and the procedure that generated it.

01

Traceable

Underlying observations link to regulatory filings. Missing values, sign conventions, and fiscal-period mismatches remain visible.

02

Point-in-time aware

Future testing must use only information available before each prediction date, including historical membership and delisted securities.

03

Falsifiable

Hypotheses, benchmarks, evaluation criteria and research limitations are version-controlled before results are promoted to findings.

04

Continuously revisable

New SEC filings trigger observation updates; models and published conclusions are separately reviewed and versioned.
