06 / OUR METHOD

Show our work
Be clear about the gaps

You should be able to see where a figure came from, when it was available and how we used it

01

Link to the source

Company figures link to their original reports
Missing figures and different reporting dates stay visible

02

Use what was known

A fair historical test can only use information available at the time
It must include companies that later failed or left the market

03

Allow the idea to fail

Set the question and the test first
Compare against simple alternatives and report results that go against the idea

04

Keep it up to date

The update schedule checks company reports twice a week once hosting is set up
Changes to research findings need a separate review
