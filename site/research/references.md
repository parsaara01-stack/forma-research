# Methodology source registry

- U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. EDGAR APIs: https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces — bulk CompanyFacts, filings and reporting metadata.
- SEC submissions and ticker directory: https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers.json — ticker mapping. Tickers are not persistent entity identifiers.
- SEC EDGAR filing search: https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/ — manual accession-level verification.
- GitHub Pages deployment: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/using-custom-workflows-with-github-pages.
- Sloan, R. G. (1996). Do stock prices fully reflect information in accruals and cash flows about future earnings? *The Accounting Review*, 71(3), 289–315.
- Fama, E. F., and French, K. R. (2015). A five-factor asset pricing model. *Journal of Financial Economics*, 116(1), 1–22. doi:10.1016/j.jfineco.2014.10.010.
- Kaplan, S. N., and Zingales, L. (1997). Do investment-cash flow sensitivities provide useful measures of financing constraints? *Quarterly Journal of Economics*, 112(1), 169–215.
- Whited, T. M., and Wu, G. (2006). Financial constraints risk. *Review of Financial Studies*, 19(2), 531–559.
- Cooper, M. J., Gulen, H., and Schill, M. J. (2008). Asset growth and the cross-section of stock returns. *Journal of Finance*, 63(4), 1609–1651.
- Kenneth R. French Data Library: https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html — publicly accessible factor series with licensing and version caveats.

**Literature status:** This is an initial bibliography, not a completed systematic novelty review. Any novelty or causality statement requires evaluation of published competing studies before manuscript submission.
